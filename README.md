## Ongoing Research – Computational Drug Discovery

My current independent research extends my medicinal chemistry background through computational investigations of benzoxazole Schiff base derivatives.

### Molecular Docking
- Molecular docking using PyRx and SwissDock.
- Protein–ligand interaction analysis using PLIP and PoseView.
- Investigation of predicted binding interactions with COX-2 and Aurora kinases.

### Molecular Dynamics Simulations
- Molecular dynamics simulations using GROMACS.
- Protein backbone RMSD and residue-level RMSF analysis.
- Radius of gyration (Rg) and protein–ligand contact analysis.
- Comparative evaluation of protein conformational behavior.

### ADMET and Toxicity Prediction
- Physicochemical and drug-likeness evaluation using SwissADME.
- Pharmacokinetic and toxicity prediction using ADMETlab 3.0 and ADMET-AI.

### Quantum Chemical Investigations
- Density functional theory (DFT) calculations using ORCA.
- HOMO–LUMO and molecular electrostatic potential analysis.

### Research Status
Ongoing computational investigations and manuscript preparation. Compound-specific unpublished findings are not disclosed in this public repository.
